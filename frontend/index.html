Company profile

Built on trust
since 1938

For 88 years, BSC has been a trusted name woven into the lives of generations of families across Karnataka. Rooted in a legacy of tradition, quality and an unwavering commitment to its customers, BSC has grown from a humble clothing store in Davanagere into a multi-city fashion and lifestyle destination — while staying true to the values it was built on.

Nearly nine decades ago, BSC began with nothing more than a pushcart and a promise. Our founder travelled from village to village across Karnataka, selling clothes and carrying with him a simple but unwavering belief: every customer deserves the very best in quality, service and trust.

In 1938, that belief took root with the opening of BSC’s first store in Davanagere. From those early days, the brand became known for its wide-ranging collections, the warmth and comfort customers felt with every visit, and a quality they could always rely on. Before long, BSC had become a household name across Davanagere — a store families turned to, time and again.

Over the decades, BSC has evolved gracefully with changing times and customer needs. From a traditional textile store, it has grown into a thriving fashion and lifestyle destination — offering clothing, accessories, occasion shopping and home essentials for every member of the family across three prominent cities in Karnataka: Davanagere, Belagavi and Shivamogga.

Today, nearly a century later, the founding family continues to lead BSC across generations, with the 5th generation now actively shaping the future of the business — carrying forward the same values of trust, quality and genuine customer-first service that built the brand.
