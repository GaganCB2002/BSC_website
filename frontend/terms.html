What this site collects, and why

We run a small, self-hosted counter to learn which collections and pages families find useful. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we ask for your consent first. If you decline, nothing is recorded — the request may appear again while you browse, and you may accept any time.

Your controls

  • Decline in the prompt and no analytics data leaves your browser for this visit.
  • Email & location are separate, optional steps. Skipping them changes nothing about browsing.
  • Location uses your browser’s own permission dialog; we store district and state, not a movement trail. No location is ever read silently.
  • Ask us to view or delete your data at any time — visit any BSC store or write to the floor manager (“Website data request”). We will act within 30 days as the DPDP Act requires.
  • Data stays on BSC’s own servers in India. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use third-party ad trackers, cookies for advertising, or fingerprinting.

Store terms (online info only)

  • Store timings shown (10:00 AM – 10:00 PM, daily) are indicative; festive days may vary.
  • This website is informational — prices and stock are confirmed in store. Tailoring and occasion-shopping services are arranged at the store counter.
  • Photographs/illustrations are representative. Return & exchange terms follow the printed bill you receive at purchase.
